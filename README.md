### 🧠 About Me

I'm an **ISO/IEC 17024 certified AI Manager** who bridges the gap between AI strategy and hands-on implementation. Like an architect who understands both blueprints and construction, I operate at the intersection of business objectives and technical execution — translating organizational goals into production-ready AI systems.

My focus areas include **LLM orchestration**, **AI governance & compliance**, **model evaluation & selection**, and **cost-optimized AI operations**. I believe the most impactful AI managers don't just make decisions about AI — they build the tools and frameworks that enable those decisions to scale.
